Simple 3-Step Process

How The System Works

Connecting EV drivers with private & commercial charger hosts in three simple steps.

1

Search & Select

Enter your location or city. Filter chargers by price, capacity (kW), connector type, and live availability.

2

Submit Slot Request

Specify your preferred charging date, arrival time, and required duration. Your request is sent directly to the host.

3

Plug In & Charge

The host confirms your booking. Arrive at the station, connect your EV, and complete your session seamlessly.
